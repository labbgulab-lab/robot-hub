## The lab
- Name: LabaLab, the Language, Affect and Behaviour in AI Research Lab (in Hebrew: המעבדה לשפה, רגש והתנהגות בבינה מלאכותית).
- Head: Dr. Guy Laban (ד"ר גיא לבן), Principal Investigator, a professor at Ben-Gurion University. He is one of the leading researchers in his field, human-robot and human-AI interaction, highly respected, with many research partnerships and collaborations. His research focuses on language, affect and behaviour in AI.
- Where: Department of Industrial Engineering and Management, Ben-Gurion University of the Negev (אוניברסיטת בן-גוריון בנגב), Beer-Sheva, Israel. Building 15, room -132.
- What it studies: how people interact with, perceive and form relationships with AI systems, at the meeting point of language, emotion (affect) and behaviour.
- Research areas: human-AI interaction, affective computing, human-robot interaction, social robots, the psychology of relationships with AI, conversational AI.
- Robots in the lab: Pepper, NAO, Furhat and Reachy Mini.

## Projects (all active)
- Robotic social cognition training for autistic adults: a coaching program with an expressive robot (Furhat) that builds emotion recognition and perspective-taking skills for job interviews and workplace communication.
- Conversational AI for real-time clinical case formulation: a conversational platform, deployed through LEXI, that tracks case formulations in therapy and supports low-effort monitoring of PTSD, anxiety and depression for clinicians and patients.
- Psychosocial safety for LLM agents: persona-driven dialogue simulation with multi-agent safety evaluation, to stress-test language models and support their safer use in sensitive conversations.
- AUT-Guard: expert-grounded AI for autism inclusion and responding to misinformation; it gives youth group leaders guidance and tools to counter autism- and disability-related misinformation online.
